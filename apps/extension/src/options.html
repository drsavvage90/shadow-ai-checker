Shadow AI Checker

by Vault Systems

What is stored

This browser keeps anonymous counts: how often a known AI site was opened, and how often a paste warning fired for a pattern such as an SSN. It does not store page text, prompts, or the pasted text. This version does not send counts anywhere.

Employers who install this on managed browsers should tell staff first. New York, Connecticut, and Delaware have electronic-monitoring notice laws, and other states may too. A template is in EMPLOYEE_NOTICE.md in the project. Have counsel review it before you rely on it.

The extension does not cover browsers or devices where it is not installed, including personal phones.
