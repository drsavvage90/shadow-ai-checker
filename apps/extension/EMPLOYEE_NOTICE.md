# Employee notice template — Shadow AI Checker

Have counsel review this before you use it. It is a starting point for firms that install the Shadow AI Checker browser extension on work browsers. New York Civil Rights Law § 52-c, Connecticut General Statutes § 31-48d, and Delaware Code title 19, § 705 require notice before certain electronic monitoring. Other states and your own policies may require notice too. This template does not make a notice sufficient.

---

**[Firm name]**  
Notice of electronic monitoring on work browsers  
**[Date]**

[Firm name] may install Shadow AI Checker, a browser extension, on firm-managed Chrome, Edge, or Firefox browsers used for work.

On those browsers, the extension:

- Records a count when the browser opens a known AI website, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or Perplexity. The count is by product name. The extension does not keep the page address, the prompt, or a copy of the page.
- When someone pastes text into one of those sites, checks the paste on that device for patterns such as Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, and simple phrases that suggest health information or a client matter.
- If a pattern matches, shows a warning on the screen and adds to a count of that pattern type.
- Does not keep the pasted text, does not read email, does not log keystrokes, and does not capture the screen.
- Does not send counts or page contents to [Firm name] over the network in the version we are installing. Counts stay in the browser profile. Someone with access to that work computer can open the extension and see the counts.
- Does not run on personal phones, home computers, or other browsers where we have not installed it.

We are using this to help reduce the chance that client, patient, or customer information is pasted into an AI tool. It does not decide whether the firm has met a legal duty.
